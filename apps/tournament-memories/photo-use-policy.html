Photo Use & Privacy Notice

Last updated November 9, 2026 · Authorization version TXSCTP_ON_THE_RANGE_2026_11_09_V1

This notice explains what Texas SCTP, Inc. asks adults to authorize when submitting tournament photographs to On the Range. It supplements other applicable Texas SCTP policies and any separately required team, organization, or parent/guardian releases.

1. Who may submit

Only adults age 18 or older who own the photographs or have the copyright owner's permission to submit them may use the photo submission form. The uploader must have permission for each identifiable person shown, including any required parent or legal guardian permission for minors. A coach or team representative role does not independently authorize a minor's image to be published.

2. Required website authorization

By actively selecting the required website checkbox, the uploader grants Texas SCTP, Inc. nonexclusive, royalty-free permission to reproduce, reasonably crop, display, and publish the submitted photographs and accompanying captions on its public Texas SCTP On the Range website after staff review. The copyright owner retains ownership. Submission does not guarantee publication.

3. Separate, optional Facebook and Instagram authorization

The uploader may select a different, optional checkbox authorizing Texas SCTP, Inc. to choose approved photographs and captions for its official Texas SCTP Facebook and Instagram pages to showcase youth shooting sports, teams, athletes, and tournaments. Texas SCTP staff separately reviews images, captions, and permission before queueing. Declining optional social-media consent does not affect eligibility for the gallery. Social posts may be viewed, copied, or reshared by other people, including parties outside Texas SCTP's control.

4. Content review and safety

Texas SCTP may approve, decline, edit captions, reasonably crop, withhold, or remove submissions. Do not include private addresses, personal contact details, or other sensitive information about athletes in captions. Photos involving minors must have appropriate permissions before submission and may require additional review. Permission to post a photo does not authorize unrelated advertising or third-party use on behalf of sponsors.

5. Information we collect

The uploader supplies a full name, email address, relationship to the images, an adult confirmation, gallery permission, and an optional social-media choice. Texas SCTP records the accepted disclosure version, permission choices, date and time, and submission identifiers. Anti-abuse controls may generate a one-way pseudonymous request fingerprint. These authorization records are for verification, moderation, and handling of rights or removal concerns.

The uploader's name, email address, relationship, and authorization details are not displayed in the public gallery. Access is limited to authorized Texas SCTP personnel. Texas SCTP retains authorization evidence for as long as reasonably necessary to document permitted publication and respond to questions or legal obligations.

6. Correction, removal, and withdrawal of permission

To request correction, removal, or withdrawal of permission for future uses, email info@texassctp.com and identify the photo, event, team, and your relationship to it. Texas SCTP will review requests and take appropriate action on content under its control. Copies previously copied, cached, downloaded, or reshared by others may remain accessible.

7. Contact and additional permissions

For questions about photography rights, parental permissions, youth privacy, or authorization records, contact info@texassctp.com. Separate written releases may be appropriate for identifiable minors, publicity campaigns, commercial advertising, or uses beyond the specific website and official Texas SCTP social-media pages described above.

Before you submit: The website checkbox is required and social-media consent is optional. Both are initially unchecked. The uploader must be an authorized adult who can make the statements being accepted.
