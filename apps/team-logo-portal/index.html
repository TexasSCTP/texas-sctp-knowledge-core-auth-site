WHY UPLOAD YOUR TEAM LOGO?

Make sure your team is represented the right way.

When Texas SCTP highlights a team or its athletes, we should have the right artwork ready. Here's what putting your official logo on file does for your program.

01

Put your team on the map

Your logo appears in the statewide team directory, giving coaches, families, and other programs a recognizable identity to find.

02

Be ready for team recognition

Having your official logo available helps Texas SCTP prepare tournament announcements, team spotlights, and athlete achievement graphics with the correct branding.

03

Keep the right version on file

Submit your team's real artwork once, and replace it whenever it changes. Help avoid outdated versions, guessed designs, and repeated requests for the same file.

SUBMITTING A LOGO

What happens after you upload?

Your logo appears on your team's directory card, and Texas SCTP has the correct file available for future team features and promotional graphics. Upload a clear PNG, JPG, or WebP image. A transparent background works best. You can replace an outdated logo without losing the previous file.
